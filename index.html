How to play NIM Jungle

  1. Start at 0 (or the challenge number).
  2. On your turn, click a glowing stone or use the +1 / +2 buttons to add to the total.
  3. Players take turns. Try to reach 10 exactly.
  4. The player who lands on the target wins!

Watch the animals leap, follow the camera, and look for the pattern. Can you discover a winning strategy?
